Onboard a customer and the simulator screens them and every beneficial owner against sanctions and PEP lists, scores the risk, applies CDD or EDD and enforces who may decide: analyst, senior analyst, MLRO or auditor. Every rule is mapped to the Irish Criminal Justice Act 2010 and the EU AML rulebook, and every action lands in an append only audit trail.

Educational portfolio project. All sanctions and PEP entries are fictional sample data. Do not enter real personal data: cases are stored only in this browser.

Ongoing CDD: periodic and event driven reviews

Approved customers are reviewed every 12, 24 or 36 months by risk tier, and immediately when something material changes (s.33, s.35(3)). A review re-screens the customer, re-checks the documents on the review date and needs a new decision.

Exceptions register

A documented deviation from policy, with a mitigating control and a deadline of at most 90 days. Raised from the case, approved by a senior analyst or the MLRO who did not raise it. An exception never unlocks a blocked case: incomplete CDD and open sanctions hits still block approval.

Quality assurance

Every High risk decision and 1 in 4 of the other decisions go to a QA check by a senior analyst or the MLRO who did not take the decision. Failed checks go back to the analyst and feed the error trends on the dashboard.

Access control (IAM)

Security model

Access is enforced by the rules engine on every action, not only by hiding buttons. The model combines role based access control (RBAC) with attribute based rules (ABAC) that look at the case risk tier, its status, who prepared it and whether the user holds delegated authority.

Least privilegeAnything not explicitly allowed is denied. Analysts approve only Low and Medium risk cases.
Segregation of dutiesInternal audit is read only. Only the MLRO decides escalated cases, manages watchlists and grants authority.
Four eyes principleSenior management approval for PEP and high risk country cases must come from a different person than the case preparer.
Delegated authorityNew analysts start without approval rights. The MLRO grants it once competency is shown, and the grant is logged.
AccountabilityEvery case action records the user and role in the audit trail. Sign ins and privilege changes go to the access log.

Sign in is simulated with a user switcher. A production system would use single sign on with MFA, and enforce the same rules on the server.

    Policy summary

    Every rule in this simulator maps to a section of the written policy (docs/POLICY.md) and to Irish or EU law. The main reference is the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010, as amended, which transposes the EU AML Directives. The new EU single rulebook, Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 (AMLR), applies from 10 July 2027.
